## 2. Tracing the field

- VERIFIED: the exact text is the GSM characteristic `included in case -control study: yes|no`
  (note the space before the hyphen). It occurs in every sample record of both AddNeuroMed batches,
  GSE63060 (155 yes / 174 no) and GSE63061 (142 yes / 246 no). It occurs nowhere else in the local
  metadata. All 329 GSE63060 records share one processing statement ("Using LUMI package in R").
- REPORTED (GEO series records, both batches): "The design is case-control. Cases are either
  Alzheimer's disease patients, subjects with mild cognitive impairment or age and gender matched
  controls." Contributors: A. Hodges, R. Howard. PubMed 26343147. SubSeries of GSE63063.
- VERIFIED: **no local text defines what `yes` or `no` means.**

## 4. What the flag corresponds to in the recorded data

### 4.1 Observed regularities (VERIFIED in both batches)

**Age ceiling.** Every "yes" sample is aged 75 or under; every sample over 75 is "no".

| | Over 75: yes / no | 75 or under: yes / no | Age range of "yes" |
|---|---|---|---|
| GSE63060 | 0 / 146 | 155 / 28 | 58–75 |
| GSE63061 | 0 / 237 | 142 / 9 | 57–75 |

**Recruitment-code concordance.** Participant codes (GSM `description`) carry a group letter in most
records: `KPOA…`, `KPOC…`, `KPOM…` in batch 1, and `KPOADC…`, `LDZCTL…`, `THSMCI…` in batch 2. In
GSE63060:

- 97 of 97 "A"-coded participants are AD, 81 of 82 "C"-coded are controls, and 70 of 107 "M"-coded
  are MCI.
- The exceptions are **37 AD participants with an "M" code** and 1 MCI participant with a "C" code.
- 43 codes (site `LND`) carry no group letter.
- **No "yes" sample has a code that disagrees with its GEO diagnosis.** All 18 samples aged 75 or
  under with a discordant code are "no"; 17 of them are M-coded AD.

**Necessary, not sufficient.**

| | Samples meeting both conditions | of which "yes" | of which "no" | "yes" failing a condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GSE63060 | 165 | 155 | 10 (2 AD, 2 CTL, 6 MCI) | 0 |
| GSE63061 | 151 | 142 | 9 | 0 |

The two conditions are age 75 or under, and a code group (where present) that agrees with the
diagnosis. Listings of the unexplained "no" samples:
`*_flag_no_despite_meeting_observed_conditions.csv`. In GSE63060 the two control exceptions are the
two youngest controls (ages 52 and 54; KPOC0001, KPOC0002). The other eight have no distinguishing
recorded attribute.

**Other attributes.** Sex, ethnicity, site and chip do not separate the groups: the flag varies
within 29 of 29 chips in GSE63060 and 33 of 34 in GSE63061. In GSE63061 all six ambiguous or
transition labels ("borderline MCI" ×3, "CTL to AD", "MCI to CTL", "OTHER") are "no". Five of them are
over 75; the sixth (GSM1539750, borderline MCI, age 75) is one of the 9 unexplained "no" samples,
although its ambiguous label may be the reason.

### 4.3 Interpretation

- INTERPRETATION: "yes" most plausibly marks the subset analysed as a case-control comparison in
  the associated publication. Its observable footprint matches an eligibility rule of age 75 or under
  and a diagnosis unchanged from the recruitment group (for example, excluding MCI-recruited
  participants later diagnosed with AD). It also matches the series' description of age-matched
  controls: mean ages in the "yes" subset are 69.9 (AD) and 69.6 (controls), versus 75.4 and 72.4
  overall.
- UNRESOLVED: the reason for the 10 + 9 remaining "no" samples (possibilities include matching,
  array QC or outlier removal, or consent), whether the age bound is exactly 75, and whether the
  group letter really encodes the recruitment group. These are inferred from naming patterns; the
  coding scheme is not documented locally.
- UNRESOLVED: whether the "yes" selection used expression-data quality criteria. If it did, it would
  be a data-dependent selection that should be reproduced explicitly, not inherited.

## 5. Implications for the AD definition (relevant to any population choice)

- VERIFIED: 37 of 145 GEO "AD" samples (25.5%) have an MCI-type participant code. If the code marks
  the recruitment group (INTERPRETATION), these are MCI-to-AD converters.
- UNRESOLVED: whether the blood sample was drawn before or after their AD diagnosis. GEO gives a
  single "status" with no date. If the blood was drawn while they were MCI, labelling them AD at
  sampling would be misclassification. The flag excludes all of them.
- VERIFIED: GSE63061 also records 6 transition or ambiguous labels; other cohorts (GSE140829,
  GSE99039) do not carry this flag at all.

## 6. Publication-described exclusions and sample IDs

- UNRESOLVED: no publication text, supplementary table or sample-ID list is available locally, so no
  outlier, exclusion or mismatch IDs could be matched. Nothing was matched or excluded. The Phase 2A
  anomalies (unlabelled array `4856076038_D`; three empty-header AD columns, GSM1539292/94/98) remain
  candidates for a reported "ID mismatch", but this is not verified. All three of those AD samples are
  flagged "no", and all three fail **both** observed conditions: ages 79, 81 and 84, with MCI-type codes
  KPOM0001, KPOM0046 and PRGM0009.

## 7. Options assessed (no decision based on sample size or expression results)

| Population | Definition | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. All GEO AD vs controls (145 vs 104) | GEO `status` AD or CTL | Fully transparent; uses only recorded diagnosis; the same rule can be applied to every cohort, including GSE140829 (no flag) | Includes 37 likely MCI-to-AD converters with unknown blood-draw timing; AD older than controls (needs age handling); ignores the submitters' own case-control designation |
| B. Flagged subset (49 vs 67) | GEO flag = yes | Probably the submitters' defined, age-matched case-control set; excludes converters and over-75s | Definition undocumented and not fully reproducible (19 unexplained exclusions across the two batches); cannot be applied to cohorts without the flag; may embed unknown, possibly data-dependent selection |
| C. Explicit criteria (51 AD vs 69 controls) | AD or CTL, age 75 or under, code group not discordant with diagnosis | Reproducible and recorded; resembles B without unexplained exclusions | Derived post hoc from the flag's pattern rather than from a documented protocol; the code-letter meaning is inferred; the 75-year bound is borrowed, not justified |
| D. Defer | Check PubMed 26343147 (methods and supplement) first | Resolves the field from its source | Needs network access or a user-supplied copy |

The counts for C come from `GSE63060_flag_by_conditions.csv` and the listings: AD 49 + 2 = 51,
controls 67 + 2 = 69.

## 8. Recommendation

**D — defer the final choice until PubMed 26343147 (methods and supplementary material) and, ideally,
the SuperSeries GSE63063 record can be checked.** The local evidence shows what the flag is
*consistent with*, but not what it *means*. Its meaning determines whether option B is a protocol-based
eligibility rule (and therefore defensible) or an undocumented selection (and therefore not). The
check is narrow: confirm the definition of the case-control subset, the age limit, the handling of
converters, any outlier or QC exclusions with sample IDs, and the timing of blood draw relative to
diagnosis.

Proposed contingency, to be written into a statistical analysis plan **before** any expression
analysis (this report does not adopt it):

- **If the publication defines the flag with protocol-based, non-data-driven criteria:** use those
  criteria, written out explicitly, as the primary population. Apply the same written criteria to the
  other cohorts where their recorded fields allow it.
- **If it does not, or the selection used expression data:** primary population A (all GEO AD vs
  controls) with prespecified handling of age and sex. Prespecify the converter question as a
  sensitivity analysis (excluding the 37 MCI-coded AD), plus sensitivity analyses on populations B
  and C.
