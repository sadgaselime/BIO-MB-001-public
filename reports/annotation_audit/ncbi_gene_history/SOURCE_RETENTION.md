## Consequences

- `v1/` cannot be regenerated byte-identically from source, because the exact `gene_history.gz` is gone.
  Its outputs, hashes and the records it used remain in the project.
- A re-run with a newly downloaded `gene_history.gz` would be a new version (`v2`), not a reproduction of v1.
- Durable retention of future source files needs a location decision: `data/` is reserved for GEO
  files, and scratch storage is not durable. No location has been chosen.
